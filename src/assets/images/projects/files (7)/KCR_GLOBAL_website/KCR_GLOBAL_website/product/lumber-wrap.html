Lumber Wrap

Protective Packaging

Overview

A UV-stabilized woven polyethylene fabric designed to protect stacked lumber packs from moisture, UV degradation and staining during storage, rail transit and ocean shipment.

Get a Quote Download Datasheet
Applications

Where Lumber Wrap is used.

  • Sawmill pack wrapping
  • Export lumber container loading
  • Outdoor lumber yard storage
  • Rail and flatbed transit protection
Benefits

Why buyers specify this product.

UV-stabilized for 12+ months outdoor exposure

Water-repellent coating prevents staining

High tear resistance withstands strapping and handling

Available in standard or custom pack widths

Specifications

Technical Data Sheet

MaterialWoven PE, UV-stabilized
Weight80-100 gsm
Roll Width1.2m - 3.0m (custom available)
UV Resistance12+ months
Tensile Strength≥ 35 lbf (ASTM D882)
Water RepellencyAATCC 22 spray rating 90+
Packaging

How it ships.

Supplied in large rolls on cardboard cores, individually wrapped, palletized for container loading.
