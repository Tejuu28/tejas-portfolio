Inside the Facility

From raw resin to a tested, packed roll.

Production Flow

Six stages, one continuous quality record.

01

Raw Material Inspection

Incoming resin and yarn tested for consistency before entering production.

02

Extrusion / Weaving

Fabric base formed on calibrated extrusion or weaving lines to target GSM.

03

Coating / Lamination

Functional layers applied for waterproofing, UV resistance or breathability.

04

In-Line Quality Checks

Tensile strength, thickness and width verified at set production intervals.

05

Slitting & Roll Finishing

Product slit and rolled to final specification and packaging length.

06

Pre-Dispatch Testing & Packing

Final batch testing, labelling and export-grade packing before container loading.
