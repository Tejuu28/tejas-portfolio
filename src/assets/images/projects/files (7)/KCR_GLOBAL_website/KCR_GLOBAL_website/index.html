About KCR Global

Engineering material performance for buyers who can't afford to inspect before they order.

KCR Global Pvt Ltd manufactures synthetic roofing underlayment, house wrap and a wide range of industrial packaging products from our integrated production facility. Every roll is produced to consistent GSM, tensile strength and UV-resistance specifications, tested before dispatch, and documented for customs and quality clearance in the destination country.

Our Mission

Deliver consistent, export-grade synthetic building and packaging materials that international buyers can trust sight unseen.

Our Vision

To be the manufacturing partner of choice for importers building long-term supply relationships from India.

FAQ

Common questions from first-time buyers.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
MOQ varies by product — typically one full container load (FCL) for standard rolls, with lower quantities available for sample and trial orders.
Can you produce to our own specification?
Yes. We regularly customise GSM, width, roll length, colour and lamination to match a buyer's technical specification or private-label requirement.
Do you provide samples before a bulk order?
Yes, sample rolls are available on request, generally shipped by courier with cost adjusted against a confirmed order.
